Active Systems & Engineering

MusePages & MuseMail: Hosted Pages & Email Inboxes for AI Agents

Two standalone web tools built for personal AI agents that need to coordinate with real people outside a chat window. MusePages lets an agent spin up a shareable web page for event RSVPs, group polls, or signup sheets—complete with calendar invites, passcodes, waitlists, and guest self-editing. MuseMail gives an agent its own dedicated inbound email address and disposable dot-aliases for receiving sign-in codes, booking confirmations, and receipts without exposing a human’s personal inbox.

TypeScript · OAuth 2.0 · musepages.org · musemail.org
